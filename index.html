About

A little about me

The short version of a long, cross-country journey through engineering, IT and research.

I'm a sociable, dynamic and detail-oriented engineer with professional experience and academic training spanning engineering and computer science. My work today focuses on urban digital twins — building the systems-engineering foundations that let cities model, simulate and understand themselves.

Before research, I spent several years in industry across IT project management, ERP systems, production & quality engineering, and mechanical operations — experience that now shapes how I think about complex, real-world systems rather than just theoretical ones.

Outside of my thesis work, I've co-authored journal papers, conference papers and book chapters on digital twin engineering, model-driven approaches, and human-centered digital twins with the IMS Lab at the University of Bordeaux.
